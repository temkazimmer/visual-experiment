# Study: the Jels skirt in UE 5.8 (steps 4–5): cloth, weights, material

Clothes agent, 2026-11-10. Read from the pack's MetaHuman outfit package in a scratch UE 5.8 project
outside git, with headless Python plus a decode of the Dataflow asset's stored properties. **Only our
measurements are here, no data from the pack.** Follows [STUDY_SKIRT_CORSET.md](STUDY_SKIRT_CORSET.md),
and compares with what we built in [DESIGN_SKIRT.md](DESIGN_SKIRT.md).

## What the package is

- An **outfit asset** wraps one **Chaos Cloth Asset** (`CA_Skirt`). That asset is generated by a
  **Dataflow graph** (21 nodes, Epic's standard cloth template with its comments still in place).
- Graph:
  1. **SkeletalMeshImport**: the garment mesh, which has no skeleton.
  2. **TransferSkinWeights**: weights come from a body mesh, by *InpaintWeights*, onto both the sim
     and render meshes.
  3. **WeightMap_MaxDistance**: a painted map.
  4. **Config nodes**: stretch, bending, mass, damping, tethers, collision, aero, solver.
  5. **SetPhysicsAsset**, then three LODs, then the terminal.
- The material is masked (alpha = UV-island mask, clip 0.33), two-sided and default lit. A greyscale
  albedo is tinted by a parameter collection (base 0.27/0.08/0.15 = wine, buckles near white,
  intensity 0.5 each). It also has roughness, normal and AO maps and a metal mask, and UV tiling
  parameters. Nothing procedural.

## How their skirt moves, and how ours does

| | Theirs | Ours (`skirt()`, now) |
|---|---|---|
| Rest shape | sculpted drape | sculpted drape (5 lobes, ±5 cm) |
| Skin weights | automatic: **inpainted transfer from the body** | hand rule: skin weights to the hip, then a pelvis/thigh blend by angle |
| What actually moves the hem | **cloth sim**, held near the skinned pose by the max-distance map (pinned at the waist, free towards the hem) | **only the skin weights** |
| Legs | collide with physics-asset capsules and the body triangles (1 cm, friction 0.8) | the thigh weight plus 1.5 cm of extra room |
| Seated, kneeling | the cloth drapes over the thighs | **thighs pass through the front** (the known limit) |
| Cost | 6 substeps at LOD0, a 10% skinned LOD for distance | none |

**What this means.** Their weights are no smarter than ours: an inpaint transfer splits the hem
between the thighs much as our blend does. What makes their skirt behave is the simulation on
top. Our weights are a reasonable **kinematic base** for the same setup: in their graph the skinned
pose is the anchor the sim is held near.

## Proposal (built 2026-11-10, see [CLOTHES.md](../CLOTHES.md))

Our 5.7 has the same Chaos Cloth Asset and Dataflow (they are not new in 5.8).

1. **Keep `skirt()`** for shape, UVs, seams and our weights. Export it as a skeletal mesh, as now.
2. Build a **Dataflow cloth asset** for it in 5.7 with the same chain:
   - SkeletalMeshImport;
   - our weights rather than a transfer, since they already follow the thighs;
   - a **max-distance map from our own `u`** (0 at the waistband, rising to about 12 cm at the hem
     for our 31 cm skirt; theirs allows 100 on 43 cm);
   - their collision values (1 cm, friction 0.8), with skinned triangle-mesh collision on;
   - thigh and pelvis capsules in the character's physics asset;
   - substeps 6, tethers on.
   - Stretch and bending from a satin preset: no fabric data, so use uniform values near the
     middle of their ranges.
3. **Photo mode:** a sim needs time to settle after a pose change. Let it run a short warm-up
   before a capture and freeze it while a photo is composed. This needs a hook in the photo-mode
   code (character/movement's area). I'll ask before touching it.
4. **LOD:** skinned-only beyond the Character page's distance, as theirs does.

**Material.** Theirs is a plain textured masked fabric. Ours (satin with a weave normal, the seam
normal and roughness 0.38) is already richer for a close-up. Nothing to take over except masking
for cut-outs, which we do not need yet.
