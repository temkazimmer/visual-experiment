## How to use this

- **Movement agent (`character-animation`):** read GUIDELINES, then ANIMATION and RIG. Use the study workflow on each pack Tema buys, and log what you learn in GUIDELINES. Check RIG's pitfalls list (catwalk layer doubling, `PhotoGait`, walk speed in three places) before adding any clip.
- **Character-design agent:** read ANATOMY and the character-design section of BUY_LIST. Use the joint table for correctives.
- **Clothes agent:** read ANATOMY's bust/hip/shoulder section and RIG's notes on weights and shape keys.
- **Main agent:** read BUY_LIST for what Tema may purchase, and GUIDELINES' review checklist for sign-off.

The aim is to **learn and build the knowledge into our own model, not paste assets in.** Prices and listings change: re-check before buying. Items I could not verify are listed at the bottom of BUY_LIST.
